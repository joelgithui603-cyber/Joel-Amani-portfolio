My top 5 movies & TV series

These are five screen favourites I enjoy watching, from crime investigations and action teams to superhero adventures.

01 · Crime / Investigation

NCIS

I enjoy the investigations, teamwork and problem-solving as the team works through complex cases.

02 · Superhero / Action

Spider-Man

I enjoy the superhero action, exciting adventures and the message of responsibility and helping others.

03 · Action / Police drama

SWAT

The teamwork, high-pressure missions and action make this one of my favourite series to watch.

04 · Military / Action drama

SEAL Team

I like the focus on discipline, courage, teamwork and the challenges faced during missions.

05 · Crime / Investigation

FBI

The investigations, suspense and coordination involved in solving cases keep me interested.

Why I enjoy these shows and films

Many of my favourites feature teamwork, critical thinking, determination and people working together to solve difficult problems.
